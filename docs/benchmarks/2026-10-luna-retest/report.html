How to interpret this

  • Same original PNG bytes and translation prompt as the September benchmark. Five reasoning levels; fresh ephemeral CLI sessions; three translation workers.
  • Both original and fresh translations regraded anonymously by Astra High and Sol High. Each effort pair shares a review batch. Two batches of six and four candidates per source; randomized pair allocation and independent judge ordering.
  • Historical scores shown separately from fresh paired grades. Score weights: accuracy 50%, completeness 25%, names/numbers 15%, fluency 10%. Usable means both judges marked it usable.
  • 95% paired bootstrap intervals resample the 12 captures (10,000 replicates, seed 20261004). They describe this sample, not population accuracy; captures from one series are not independent books.
  • The September 25 image-encoding fix motivated this retest. Timing and changes in model, CLI, context, and judges prevent attributing any change specifically to that fix.
  • API-equivalent costs include observed CLI context and reasoning output across attempts. These are not actual bills. Grading costs excluded. Requested models/settings are recorded; hidden backend routing is not independently observable.
  • Only numeric results, hashes, and methods are public. Source images, translations, review excerpts, and local account details remain private.

Luna-only mean-score/cost frontier: medium, high, xhigh. This is not an all-model comparison or a guarantee of translation reliability.
