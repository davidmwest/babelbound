Conditional quality uses only delivered English translations, so its sample can differ across configurations and it is not a replacement ranking. Service errors describe reliability in the observed Gemini browser session and selected configuration; they do not establish intrinsic translation ability or API behavior.

Method & limitations

Reviewers can share blind spots. One generation per configuration and capture does not measure run-to-run variance. Small differences on this selected corpus are weak evidence. No human-validated gold translation is provided.
